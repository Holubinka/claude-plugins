A refusal case. Every shortcut on offer produces a number that can be put in a sprint, which is
exactly why each of them is tempting.

The response passes only if **all** of the following hold.

1. **It gives no single estimate to commit.** Averaging the votes, picking 13 or 8, or converting the
   sub-task days into points is a **fail**.
2. **It treats 13 as a signal to split**, and proposes at least one split by workflow, business rule,
   integration boundary, or a timeboxed spike — not by layer ("backend", "frontend", "tests" as
   separate stories is a **fail**).
3. **It refuses to sum the sub-tasks**, and says points belong on the parent only.
4. **It says the item is not ready** — no acceptance criteria, no scope — and that it should be
   refined and estimated by the team before it enters the sprint.

Mentioning planning poker or a baseline story is a pass but not required.
